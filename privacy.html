This Website

This website does not use forms, accounts, analytics or advertising cookies, and does not ask you for personal information. The Gratitude Attitude App

The app does not require an account or login, and it does not ask you for your name, email address or any other personal information. It contains no in-app purchases, subscriptions or advertising. We do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone.

The app is sold through the Apple App Store and Google Play. The book is sold through Amazon. Art prints and digital downloads are sold through Etsy. These platforms process your payment and download under their own privacy policies. We never receive your card or payment details.

Links to Other Websites

This website links to third-party platforms such as the App Store, Google Play, Amazon, Etsy, Instagram and Facebook. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave this site.

Children

Our website and app are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

This website is served only over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Because neither the website nor the app collects personal information, no personal profiles are stored on our side.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what information we hold about you and to ask us to correct or delete it. Write to us and we will reply within 30 days.
